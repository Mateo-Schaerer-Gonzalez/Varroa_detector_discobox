    Live from the Discobox

    A test run as the Discobox app runs it: every few minutes the fan and LEDs come on and the camera records a burst of frames. Each recording is analysed as soon as it is in, and the result pages fill in as the run goes.

    Discobox

    Calibration

    For each detected mite and each recording, you watch the clip and mark whether the mite moves. The detector's calls are then compared with yours, one mite-recording at a time.

    Saved ground truth

    Every recording you label is kept here: its ground truth and a copy of its recordings, never its scores. Go on labelling it later, or pool several into one report for more data; each report scores them again with the movement score you choose. Tick the datasets to pool.

    Watch the clip and mark, for every mite in every recording, whether it moves. Click a mite to cycle it through moving, still, not a mite and back to unlabelled; Shift-click goes backwards. Not a mite applies to every recording and removes the detection from later analyses of this folder; clicking on past a not a mite you just set brings back the mite's labels. ↑ ↓ change recording, ← → change zone, P plays or pauses.

    Ground truth

    The detector's own call is hidden until the report, so it cannot sway you. Save changes or showing the report saves your labels, also to the saved ground truth you can pool later.

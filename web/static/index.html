    Calibration

    For each detected mite and each recording, you watch the clip and mark whether the mite moves. The detector's calls are then compared with yours, one mite-recording at a time.

    Saved ground truth

    Every recording you label is kept here: its ground truth and a copy of its recordings, never its scores. Go on labelling it later, or pool several into one report for more data; each report scores them again with the movement score you choose. Tick the datasets to pool.
